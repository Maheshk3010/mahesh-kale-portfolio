# Mahesh Kale Premium Data Product Redesign

## Goal
Reshape the existing portfolio into an evidence-first analytical story for only **Data Analyst** and **MIS Executive** roles, while preserving the current TanStack foundation, MAHI.AI, accessibility behavior, verified knowledge, and working interactions.

## Information architecture
1. Hero
2. Analytics Snapshot
3. Selected Case Studies
4. MIS & Reporting
5. Field Experience
6. Capability Stack
7. Education & Credentials
8. Behind the Analysis
9. Final CTA
10. Footer

MAHI.AI remains floating and secondary. Remove the standalone workflow chapter; its useful sequence becomes the narrative thread connecting Hero, case studies, and MIS.

## Build changes

### Cinematic opening and proof
- Recompose the Hero around the exact identity: **Mahesh Kale / Data Analyst / MIS · Reporting · BI**.
- Keep the headline immediate while animating a restrained Data → Validate → Analyze → KPI → Dashboard → Report sequence.
- Move proof into a dedicated compact analytics snapshot directly below the opening.
- Use only verified metrics already stored: 50K+ retail records, 7K+ customer records, 3 internships, and 30+ SQL queries. Do not use the proposed 15+ KPI count because it is not verified.
- Keep View Case Studies primary. Keep Download Resume visibly unavailable until a verified file exists; never replace it with “Request Resume.”

### Evidence-first case studies
- Rename the project chapter to **Selected Case Studies** and make it the dominant editorial section.
- Present three evidence tracks: Sales Performance Analysis, Sales Dashboard, and Customer Churn Analysis.
- Structure each around Business Question, Data, Workflow, Tools, Findings, Business Output, Evidence, and links.
- Use large evidence areas rather than thumbnail cards. Where screenshots, findings, outcomes, or repository matches are absent, show concise evidence-state placeholders without pretending verification.
- Keep Apple Stock Prediction and Product Recommendation only in compact Additional Technical Work when supported by current knowledge. No invented ETL case study or URL will be presented as completed work.

### Role proof and supporting chapters
- Rebuild MIS & Reporting as an operational reporting surface with Source Data → Excel/SQL → Validation → Reconciliation → KPI Calculation → Power BI/Report → Management View, plus the verified capability areas.
- Keep Field Experience as a concise vertical evidence timeline; show missing dates, locations, and deliverables as verification gaps.
- Rework Skills into Capability Stack relationships that connect tools to documented projects, reporting tasks, and internships; machine learning stays secondary.
- Keep Education & Credentials compact, with missing years and verification links clearly marked.
- Reduce Behind the Analysis to 2–4 factual lines plus portrait/location; remove repeated role, degree, internship, and workflow panels.
- Make the final CTA a calmer cinematic close with identity, roles, availability, and verified contact links.

### Visual and motion system
- Preserve graphite/navy/cyan tokens while reducing repetitive card and glass treatment.
- Give each chapter a distinct visual grammar: atmospheric Hero, control-panel proof, editorial case studies, operational MIS, timeline Experience, relationship-map Stack, factual Credentials, quiet CTA.
- Centralize purposeful Motion animations: hero activation, one-time count-up, case-study evidence reveals, MIS flow, timeline drawing, capability relationships, and decelerating closing motion.
- Use transform/opacity/clip-path only where practical, preserve reduced-motion behavior, and simplify visuals on small screens.

## Factual safeguards
- No new metrics, dates, findings, business outcomes, screenshots, demos, repositories, certification links, or resume files.
- Repository mismatches remain unavailable rather than being mislabeled.
- Machine learning appears only as supporting technical work, never as role positioning.
- Remove any remaining Data Scientist, Python Developer, ML Engineer, or Machine Learning Practitioner positioning from visible portfolio copy and navigation.

## Validation
- Verify the 10-second first viewport: identity, both roles, four core tools, work focus, proof, and actions.
- Verify the 30-second sequence: case studies, evidence state, MIS relevance, SQL/reporting, and internships.
- Test desktop, tablet, and mobile for overflow, readable case-study hierarchy, navigation order, floating assistant overlap, and CTA access.
- Confirm keyboard navigation, focus states, reduced motion, image loading, metadata, console/network health, and successful preview build.
